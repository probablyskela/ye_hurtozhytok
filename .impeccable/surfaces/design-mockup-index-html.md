# Laundry booking app (mockup of v1 screens)

Scope: phone-first PWA screens for booking a washing machine — sign-in, home, booking flow, machine day, booking detail. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: dorm residents, one-handed in a hallway or next to the machine; find a free time on one of three machines, book it, see it, cancel it. Content: three machines, programs → lengths, 15-min grid, 15-min buffer, up to 3 bookings. Constraints: Ukrainian only, 44px targets, light, no state symbols, honest campaign credit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A booking is a document you carry, like a card in a state-services wallet: the screen is a calm glass instrument over a soft water-coloured field, and your booking is the one solid object on it. Refuses the grey admin calendar grid and the paper-notebook costume.

OWN-WORLD: Pastel field (mint #d8f3ea → sky #d9e7fb → lilac #ebe3f8) washing under white liquid-glass tiles (radius 24–28, rim light, soft long shadow). Graphite ink #101217, black capsule primary buttons, muted slate #586072. One deep ultramarine #2a37d8 for "yours" (your booking card, selected start); free time in aqua #0f8f7e; now-line coral #e5484d. Fixel Text (headings 700) with Fixel Display Black for times and duration numerals only; tabular figures only on times. Mark: black squircle with white «є» and a round porthole dot.

STORY: In one glance the resident sees their next booking, which machine is free now, and the nearest free time; two taps book it; the card tells them what happens next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home. Wordmark top-left, avatar right. Greeting line. The booking card (ultramarine gradient, porthole motif, time in 42px Fixel Display, countdown, machine) spans full width. Below it one glass panel «Пралки зараз» with three rows — porthole state dot, machine name and state, day ribbon (08–22) with coral now-marker — all above the tab bar. Then "Найближчий вільний час" duration control and chips. Bottom: glass tab bar with central black "Записатися" capsule.

FORM: Pinned by the user, so concept-seed was not run (no seed key). Authority, the user's words: «Головні 2 натхненники мій дизайн кривди та дизайн система державних застосунків України, по типу дія». Signature move: the day strip — each machine's day as a capsule ribbon with booked segments, hatched buffers and a coral now-marker; choosing a start makes your ultramarine segment glide into the ribbon.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
